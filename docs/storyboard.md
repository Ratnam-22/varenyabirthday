# Project Varenya

# Storyboard

Version: 1.0

---

# Story Overview

The experience is a cinematic interactive journey.

The user enters a magical world that slowly reveals itself.

Instead of navigating pages, the user experiences scenes.

Each scene has one purpose.

Each scene creates one emotion.

The website should feel like watching a short animated film where the user controls the pace.

Total experience duration:
Approximately 4–6 minutes.

---

# Scene Structure

Every scene must contain:

• Objective

• Emotional Goal

• Visual Style

• Camera Behaviour

• Lighting

• Music

• Sound Effects

• User Interaction

• Transition

• Mobile Behaviour

---

# Scene 01

THE INVITATION

Duration:
15–20 seconds

Emotion:
Curiosity

Purpose:
Capture attention immediately.

Description:

The screen starts completely black.

No UI.

No buttons.

No loading spinner.

Only silence for one second.

Tiny stars slowly begin appearing.

Soft ambient music fades in.

A glowing particle slowly writes:

"For Someone Special"

After a short pause...

the text disappears into particles.

The stars move apart.

The camera slowly begins travelling forward.

A constellation slowly forms.

It reveals the name

Varenya

written entirely using glowing stars.

Nothing else appears.

The user simply watches.

Interaction:

Tap anywhere.

or

Click anywhere.

Transition:

The stars explode into golden particles.

The camera flies through them.

---

# Scene 02

THE GIFT

Duration:
30 seconds

Emotion:
Wonder

Purpose:
Create the first WOW moment.

Description:

The particles slowly settle.

A floating premium gift box appears.

It rotates gently.

Soft volumetric lighting.

Floating dust particles.

Ribbon gently moves.

The camera circles around it.

Background remains dark.

Only the gift is illuminated.

The gift should feel expensive.

Interaction:

Move mouse.

The gift rotates slightly.

On mobile:

Finger movement rotates it.

Click:

The ribbon slowly unties.

Not instantly.

It should feel realistic.

Sound:

Ribbon

Paper

Magic sparkle

Transition:

The lid slowly lifts.

Golden light fills the screen.

---

# Scene 03

THE MAGIC INSIDE

Duration:
20 seconds

Emotion:
Surprise

Description:

When the box opens...

Thousands of glowing particles burst outward.

Confetti.

Sparkles.

Golden butterflies.

Flowers.

Stars.

Everything moves naturally.

The camera enters the light inside the gift.

The screen becomes completely white.

Then slowly fades.

The next world appears.

Transition:

Camera flies into the magical world.

---

# Scene 04

WELCOME TO YOUR WORLD

Duration:
30 seconds

Emotion:
Joy

Description:

A magical floating garden.

Flowers.

Lanterns.

Butterflies.

Fireflies.

Soft wind.

Floating petals.

Everything alive.

Nothing static.

The camera slowly flies through the garden.

Background music becomes brighter.

The user can slightly control the camera direction by moving the cursor.

Mobile:

Touch movement slightly influences the camera.

The world should feel alive.

Interaction:

Clicking flowers causes them to bloom.

Butterflies fly away when touched.

Fireflies gather around the cursor.

Transition:

The camera notices something glowing in the distance.

It slowly moves toward it.

---

# Scene 05

THE WISHING TREE

Duration:
30–40 seconds

Emotion:
Wonder

Purpose:
Give the user a magical interaction that feels personal.

Description:

The camera enters a peaceful clearing.

At the center stands a magnificent glowing Wishing Tree.

Its branches are covered with tiny golden lights.

The leaves gently sway.

Fireflies continuously fly around it.

The roots glow with magical energy.

Every leaf has subtle motion.

Soft floating particles surround the tree.

The tree should feel ancient and magical.

Music:

Soft piano begins.

A distant choir slowly fades in.

Lighting:

Warm golden light.

Very soft bloom.

Ambient fog.

Interaction:

The tree displays one message:

"Make a birthday wish."

When the user touches or clicks the tree...

The entire tree lights up.

Thousands of glowing leaves float upward.

The camera slowly rotates around the tree.

The stars above become brighter.

Transition:

One glowing leaf floats away.

The camera follows it.

---

# Scene 06

THE SKY OF DREAMS

Duration:
35 seconds

Emotion:
Peace

Purpose:
Slow the experience before the celebration.

Description:

The camera follows the glowing leaf.

It rises into the sky.

Clouds slowly separate.

The entire night sky appears.

Aurora begins dancing.

Thousands of stars become visible.

Occasional shooting stars cross the sky.

The moon slowly appears.

Clouds move naturally.

The user cannot fully control the camera.

Only slight parallax movement.

Interaction:

Touching stars creates tiny sparkles.

Double tapping the moon creates a meteor shower.

Desktop:

Cursor creates constellations.

Mobile:

Finger movement leaves magical particles.

Transition:

A distant light slowly appears.

It grows brighter.

---

# Scene 07

THE BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION

Duration:
40–50 seconds

Emotion:
Excitement

Purpose:
The biggest visual WOW moment.

Description:

The distant light transforms into a floating birthday celebration.

A premium birthday cake appears.

Dozens of floating lanterns surround it.

Confetti gently falls.

Golden ribbons float through the air.

The cake slowly rotates.

Beautiful candle flames dance naturally.

Small butterflies circle around it.

Everything feels magical.

Music:

The happiest section of the soundtrack.

Soft orchestral celebration.

Interaction:

Tap the cake.

The candles become brighter.

A message appears:

"Make a wish..."

If microphone permission is available:

The user may blow to extinguish the candles.

Otherwise:

Tap the candles.

The flames slowly disappear.

The entire world briefly becomes darker.

Then...

Thousands of stars appear.

Fireworks begin.

Transition:

The fireworks slowly transform into stars.

---

# Scene 08

THE LETTER

Duration:
45–60 seconds

Emotion:
Emotion

Purpose:
The emotional highlight of the entire experience.

Description:

After the fireworks...

Everything becomes calm.

Music becomes soft.

The camera slowly moves downward.

A beautiful envelope gently falls from the sky.

It lands softly.

Golden wax seal.

Elegant ribbon.

The envelope slowly opens.

A handwritten letter unfolds.

Every sentence appears as though written by an invisible pen.

No typing animation.

Real handwriting motion.

Background remains softly animated.

Nothing distracts from the message.

This is the most personal scene.

Transition:

The paper gently folds itself.

It transforms into glowing butterflies.

---

# Scene 09

THE FINAL SKY

Duration:
30 seconds

Emotion:
Peace

Purpose:
Prepare for the ending.

Description:

The butterflies fly upward.

The camera follows them.

The entire galaxy becomes visible.

Nebulae.

Stars.

Constellations.

Soft aurora.

Floating particles.

Everything moves slowly.

Music almost stops.

Only piano remains.

Interaction:

None.

Allow the user to simply enjoy the moment.

Transition:

Stars slowly begin gathering together.

---

# Scene 10

THE FINAL WISH

Duration:
30–40 seconds

Emotion:
Lasting Memory

Purpose:
Leave a memorable final impression.

Description:

Thousands of stars slowly move together.

They form:

Varenya

The letters glow.

Golden particles gently fall.

The text slowly transforms into:

Happy Birthday

The background fills with elegant fireworks.

Soft flower petals fall.

Lanterns rise.

The camera slowly moves backward.

The entire magical world becomes visible one final time.

A final message appears:

"May your year be filled with happiness, beautiful memories, and dreams that come true."

Below it:

Made with ❤️ especially for Varenya

The music slowly fades.

Only one glowing star remains.

If the user taps that final star...

A tiny hidden message appears.

"Thank you for taking this journey."

Fade to black.

End.---
